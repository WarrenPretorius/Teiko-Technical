Method and caveats
  • Two-sided Mann–Whitney U test on relative frequencies. It compares ranks, so it does not assume percentages are normally distributed and is robust to outliers.
  • Five populations are tested at once, so p-values are adjusted for multiple testing with the Benjamini–Hochberg procedure. A population is called significant when its adjusted q-value is below 0.05.
  • Each subject contributes up to three samples (days 0, 7 and 14). Pooling them treats repeated samples from one patient as independent. The per-day views use one sample per subject, and the subject-averaged test confirms the pooled result.
  • Day 0 is the most relevant view for predicting response, since it is measured before treatment can have had an effect.
